Before you sell

Bring ID. Victorian scrap metal law means we record the seller’s name, address and identification for every purchase. A driver licence is easiest.

Paid by EFT. Cash payments for scrap metal are banned in Victoria, so we pay straight into your account.

Keep hazards out of the load. Ask us first about gas bottles and lithium batteries, and drain any fuel. See what we can’t take.
