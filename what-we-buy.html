What we buy

Most things made of metal. We price by metal type, grade and weight on the day, so ask for today’s rate.

Hobson Bay and River Yarra, Admiralty chart by Henry L. Cox, published 1865. State Library Victoria, public domain.

Items we buy

  • Steel and iron

    Fridges and freezers

    Clean and empty. Keep the pipes uncut: the refrigerant is recovered by an ARC-licensed technician before processing.

  • Steel and iron

    Hot water services

    Drained and already disconnected by a plumber. Heat-pump units: tell us, as the refrigerant must be recovered first.

  • Steel and iron

    Washing machines, dryers and stoves

    Empty. No gas bottles attached.

  • Steel and iron

    Roofing iron, fencing and gates

    Already taken down. No fibro or cement sheet mixed in.

  • Steel and iron

    Shelving, racking and filing cabinets

    Empty. Pallet racking welcome.

  • Steel and iron

    Pipe, angle, beams and offcuts

    Any length. Tell us if pieces are over 6 metres.

  • Steel and iron

    Cast-iron baths, sinks and BBQs

    BBQ gas bottles removed and kept by you.

  • Steel and iron

    Bikes, frames and tools

    No e-bikes or lithium batteries.

  • Copper, brass and aluminium

    Copper pipe, wire and cylinders

    Stripped or unstripped. Clean copper fetches more.

  • Copper, brass and aluminium

    Brass taps and fittings

    Remove plastic and steel parts where you can.

  • Copper, brass and aluminium

    Aluminium window frames and sheet

    Glass removed if possible.

  • Copper, brass and aluminium

    Stainless steel sinks and troughs

    Taps can stay on.

  • Copper, brass and aluminium

    Electric motors and radiators

    Drained of fluids.

  • Cars, trailers and caravans

    Car bodies and broken-down cars

    Proof of ownership needed. Tilt-tray access required.

  • Cars, trailers and caravans

    Trailers

    Any condition. Registration papers if you have them.

  • Cars, trailers and caravans

    Old caravans

    Older caravans may contain asbestos. Tell us the build year; we may need a clearance before we can take it.

  • Cars, trailers and caravans

    Boats and boat trailers

    Ask first. Fuel tanks must be empty.

  • Machinery and farm metal

    Old farm equipment

    Ploughs, harrows, tanks and frames. Fluids drained.

  • Machinery and farm metal

    Shipping containers and site offices

    Tell us the size and how a truck gets to it.

  • Machinery and farm metal

    Car and farm wheels

    Ask about tyres still on rims.

  • Machinery and farm metal

    Warehouse shelving and steel structures

    Dismantled, or ask us about dismantling.

  • Batteries

    Lead-acid car and truck batteries

    Intact and carried upright. Not lithium.

Ask us first

  • Gas cylinders of any kind, including BBQ bottles and fire extinguishers
  • Lithium batteries, e-bikes and e-scooters
  • Metal e-waste such as computers and power tools
  • Tyres still on rims

We can’t take

  • Anything with asbestos, or suspected asbestos
  • Drums or containers with liquid or residue in them
  • Fuel tanks or engines that haven’t been drained
  • General rubbish, timber, mattresses or green waste

How we price

Metal prices move every day with the commodity markets, so we don’t publish a fixed list.

Send us photos and a rough amount and we’ll give you a guide price. The final figure comes from the weighed load, by metal and grade. Clean, sorted metal is worth more: copper with the plastic stripped off, aluminium without steel screws, brass without taps attached.
